# Zella for Android: privacy policy (draft for zellahq.com)

Play needs this at a public address. It is a draft for the owner to review and publish. It is not legal advice.

Zella records and edits video on your phone. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads.

**Your videos, voice and projects** stay on your phone. Captions, cutting, voice clean-up, background removal,
reading text on screen and export all run on the phone. Your video leaves only when you share it yourself.

**Three optional features use the internet.** Each one says so where you use it, and none is on by default.

- The Assistant. If you add your own key and send a message, what you type and a short text summary of your project,
  including caption text and the results of what it did, goes to the provider you picked (Google Gemini, Groq,
  Cerebras, OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI). If you ask it to read text on screen, the reading happens on your
  phone and the words it finds go to that provider too, with secret-looking values masked. Your video and sound are
  never sent. That provider's own privacy policy
  applies. Your key is encrypted on your phone with Android Keystore and only ever goes to that provider.
- Stock video clips. If you add your own Pexels or Pixabay key and ask for clips, each search word and your key goes
  to that site, and the clip comes back. Your video is never sent.
- Bigger caption files. A download from models.zellahq.com. Nothing about you or your video is sent.

**Purchases** go through Google Play. Zella reads from Google Play which purchases your account holds, on your phone.
Zella never sees your payment details.

**Deleting.** Delete a project in the app, or uninstall Zella, and it is gone from your phone. Zella has no copy.

**Children.** Zella is not directed at children under 13.
