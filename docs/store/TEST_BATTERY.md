# The 20 clip test battery

The brief asks for one. The owner's own clips are the point, so this is the list to fill, not a set of files. Test
media cannot live in the repo; `tools/push-test-media.sh` pushes the two public clips the automated tests use.

Talking head: 1 bright indoor, 2 dim indoor, 3 outdoor with wind, 4 noisy cafe, 5 very quiet speaker, 6 fast speaker,
7 long pauses, 8 Bangla, 9 Hindi, 10 mixed Bangla and English.
Picture: 11 busy background, 12 hair against a similar wall, 13 two people, 14 person walking, 15 portrait from a
front camera, 16 landscape from a back camera, 17 HDR from a recent phone.
Screen: 18 recording with a token and an email on screen, 19 scrolling a long page, 20 a one hour file, for memory.

For each, run Auto-Polish, Make it Viral, captions, background removal and a 1080p export. Keep what comes out.
