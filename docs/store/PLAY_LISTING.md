## Short description (80 characters at most)

Record, let on-device AI cut it, and share a video. Free, with no watermark.

## Full description

Zella is a screen recorder and video editor for your phone, made for people who have never edited a video.

Record your screen or yourself, or pick a video you already have. Tap Auto-Polish, and Zella cuts the
silences and filler words, cleans up your voice, frames you and adds music. Add captions, a look or a new
background if you like, then share it.

No account. No watermark. Your video is worked on here on your phone.

WHAT YOU GET FREE, FOR GOOD
- The complete editor and all the AI
- Captions written on your phone, including Bangla and Hindi in their own script
- 92 colour looks, 25 transitions, 85 text animations, music and sound effects
- Background removal with no green screen
- Exports up to 2 minutes at up to 1080p, with no watermark

ZELLA PRO, ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Longer exports, 4K, 60 frames a second, your own quality setting, exporting several shapes at once, saved
looks, and the tools for changing things over time, colouring one area and speed curves. Every new install
gets 7 days of Pro with nothing to pay. It ends by itself and Zella stays free.

ZELLA FOUNDER, ONE PAYMENT
Everything in Pro, three colour grades only Founders have, and every future major version free.

WHAT USES THE INTERNET
Three optional things, and each says so where you use it:
- The Assistant, if you add your own key: it sends what you type and a short text summary of your project,
  including caption text, to the provider you picked. Never your video or sound.
- Stock video clips, if you add your own free Pexels or Pixabay key: it sends a search word.
- Bigger caption downloads: a one-time file download.

Bought Zella on iPhone or Mac? Apple and Google keep purchases apart, so that purchase does not unlock Android.

## Words to keep out

"Unlimited" and "AI-powered" used loosely. "Nothing is uploaded" anywhere. Any claim about Telugu captions.
Any claim about the transparent export, which Android does not have.

## What to check before pasting

- "92 colour looks" is what everyone gets. Founder adds 3 more, for 95.
- Bangla and Hindi use the optional free download for best results. The bundled model handles them with
  lower accuracy. No phone test of Bangla has been done yet.
